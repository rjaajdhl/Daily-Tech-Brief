Incident / compromise.

Consider:
- what happened,
- when it happened,
- affected systems or scope,
- actual observed impact,
- technical path,
- sequence of actions,
- which controls worked,
- which boundary or control failed or was bypassed,
- detection and response,
- concrete remediation.

Distinguish demonstrated impact from possible impact.
Do not add a lesson or takeaway when the facts already make the significance clear.
