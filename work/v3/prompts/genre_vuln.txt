Vulnerability.

Consider:
- affected component or application,
- prerequisites,
- root cause,
- exploit path,
- trust or permission boundary involved,
- demonstrated reproduction,
- affected versions or environments,
- existing defenses and why they did or did not apply,
- real-world exploitation status,
- patch or mitigation,
- limits of the reproduction.

If multiple vulnerabilities or platform behaviors are discussed, keep them clearly separate.
A concrete reproduction may be the best opening when it explains the vulnerability more clearly than starting with the root cause.
